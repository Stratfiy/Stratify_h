Make India build faster.

India wants to make far more than it does today, and it is short of skilled hands to do it.

  • Factories

    Manufacturing is meant to reach 25% of GDP by 2035. It has sat near 15 to 17% for years.

  • Skills

    Industry will need over 30 million more skilled manufacturing workers by 2030.

A robot a shop can teach itself.

Most Indian machine shops cannot use robots today, because every new job needs a programmer. We build robots that learn a job when a worker shows it to them.

Figures above from: Policy Circle, The Machine Maker (NSDC).

nA labs exists to close that gap.

We start with one robot in one machine shop. The vision is much larger: factories that run themselves, and machines that build for space before we build in space. See the roadmap.

nA labs is the robotics arm of NAutomation Labs, a company registered in Tamil Nadu. Its sister company OrderShed supplies machined parts through partner machine shops, and those shops are where our robots go first.

We started in software.

We began by building AI agents that handle business work, under the name Decibyl. The harder problem turned out to be physical: the work in a factory that no software can reach. So we started building robots.

The agents have a job on the factory side too. They will run the planning and paperwork around the machines.

AI agents for the office. Robots for the floor. Parts for the world.

  • nA labs

    The robotics arm of NAutomation Labs. Robots and the software that teaches them.

  • OrderShed

    The parts arm of NAutomation Labs. Machined parts from partner shops, for buyers in India and Europe.

  • Decibyl

    The software arm of NAutomation Labs. AI agents for quotes, orders, scheduling and follow-ups.

The same people run all three, and the same partner shops are where our robots work first.
