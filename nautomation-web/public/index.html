The first three jobs.

Work in a machine shop that is repetitive and hard to staff, especially at night.

  • CNC machine tending

    Loading raw stock and unloading finished parts, shift after shift.

  • Pick and place

    Picking parts and placing them into fixtures, trays and machines.

  • Inspection

    Presenting parts to a camera or gauge and sorting pass from fail.

Where this goes.

This is the vision, not a list of what we do today. We work on the first step, and each step makes the next one possible.

  1. Stage 1

    Shop floors

    Robots that tend machines, handle parts and assemble.

  2. Stage 2

    Field sites

    Robots that work outdoors in heat and dust, starting with solar.

  3. Stage 3

    One brain

    Software that learns each job by demonstration and runs on every robot we build.

  4. Stage 4

    Autonomous factories

    Whole floors that run and retool themselves through software.

  5. Stage 5

    Beyond Earth

    Robots that construct and maintain buildings in orbit and on the Moon.
