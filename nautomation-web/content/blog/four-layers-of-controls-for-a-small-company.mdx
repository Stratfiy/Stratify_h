The accord signed on 29 September 2026 says safe AI needs controls and audits, in layers. The documents are written for AI companies and regulators. Here is the same idea for a company with twenty people and one accountant.

## Layer 1: Scope

*What may the agent touch?*

Write it as a list. "The procurement inbox, the vendor WhatsApp group, the purchase-order sheet." Not "our systems". If it is not on the list, the agent cannot reach it. This is the cheapest control and the one most people skip.

## Layer 2: Approvals

*What must it ask about first?*

Our rule is three words: send, spend, delete. Before an agent sends anything outside the company, commits money, or removes data, a named person approves it. Inside those limits it can work on its own: draft, compare, chase, summarise.

## Layer 3: Record

*What gets written down?*

Every action, with the time, what it did, who approved it, and what happened next. Calls get a transcript. Emails get the sent copy. The record is the thing you show the auditor, the customer, or yourself at 11 pm when something feels off.

## Layer 4: Review

*Who reads the record, and how often?*

Once a week is enough for most teams. A fifteen-minute look at what the agent did, what it asked about, and what was rejected. Rejections are the useful part: they tell you where the scope or the instructions need tightening.
