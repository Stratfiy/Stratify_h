Privacy notice.

What this site collects, why, and who handles it.

This notice covers nautomationlabs.com, operated by NAutomation Labs Private Limited, Tamil Nadu, India. Last updated 19 November 2026.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form: your name, company, email address, phone number if you give one, the kind of enquiry and your message.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry about machined parts, automation, partnering or joining the team. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who handles it

The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards your enquiry to our mailbox at office@nautomationlabs.com. Our website host keeps standard server logs, such as IP address and request details, for security.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we are in conversation with you, and delete them on request.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent us, write to office@nautomationlabs.com.
