From the shop floor to beyond Earth.

This is our vision, not a list of what we do today. Energy, factories and robots are one system, and we start with the part that appears in all three.

Concept render

It is a loop.

Each part needs the other two, and robots work in all three.

  • Power

    Solar gives the energy. Robots install the panels and keep them clean. We build those robots; we do not make panels or sell power.

  • Parts

    Autonomous factories run on that energy and make the parts: for machines, for more solar, for more robots.

  • Hands

    Robots assemble those parts and do the work. One brain learns every job and carries it to every robot.

Build for space before we build in space.

A base on the Moon needs the same three things, with almost no people. Whoever can run this loop on Earth with few people can run it there.

The order we build it in.

We are working on stage one. The rest is the direction, and no stage works without the others.

  1. Stage 1

    Shop floors

    Robots that tend machines, handle parts and assemble, starting in OrderShed partner shops. This is where the brain learns its first jobs.

  2. Stage 2

    Field sites

    The same arms on a rugged base, working in heat and dust, starting with solar.

  3. Stage 3

    One brain

    Software that learns each job by demonstration and runs on every robot we build.

  4. Stage 4

    Autonomous factories

    Whole floors that run and retool themselves. Robots do the physical work, and Decibyl, our AI agents, will plan the jobs and handle the paperwork around them.

  5. Stage 5

    Build for space

    Parts and machines made for the companies that fly. This comes before anything of ours leaves the ground.

  6. Stage 6

    Build in space

    Robots that construct and maintain buildings in orbit and on the Moon.

Where we start: with the shops we already work with.

OrderShed is our sister company. It supplies machined parts to buyers in India and Europe through a network of partner machine shops.

Those partner shops are where our robots go first. The shop gets a robot it can teach itself. We learn each job on a real floor. Buyers get parts made the same way every time.

  • Shops

    Partner shops run more hours with the people they have.

  • Robots

    Every shift on a real floor teaches the brain a little more.

  • Parts

    OrderShed buyers get steadier quality and delivery.

Why the direction is right.

India is aiming for 500 GW of non-fossil power by 2030 and stood at 300.5 GW in July 2026. ISRO plans a space station by 2035 and Indians on the Moon by 2040.

SpaceX flew 25 orbital launches in 2020 and 165 in 2025. The whole world flew about 330 last year, a record. In July 2026 an Indian startup reached orbit for the first time. Everything on those rockets was built in a factory first.

Figures from: Mercom India, Outlook Business, Space.com, 2025 in spaceflight, Spherical Insights.
