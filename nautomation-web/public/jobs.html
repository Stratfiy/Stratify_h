The jobs we are building them for.

Work that is repetitive, heavy or done in hard conditions.

Concept render

On the shop floor.

Someone has to stand at the machine all night to load one part every few minutes. These are the jobs like that.

  • CNC machine tending

    Loads raw stock and unloads finished parts, shift after shift.

    Station arm, trolley arm

  • Pick and place

    Picks parts and places them into fixtures, trays and machines.

    Station arm, trolley arm

  • Assembly

    Fits, inserts and fastens parts, with one arm holding and the other working.

    Mobile two-arm

  • Kitting and bin picking

    Picks mixed parts from bins and builds kits for the next station.

    Station arm, mobile two-arm

  • Moving parts between machines

    Carries trays from one station to the next across the floor.

    Mobile two-arm

  • Inspection

    Presents parts to a camera or gauge and sorts pass from fail.

    Station arm, trolley arm

  • Palletizing

    Stacks boxes and finished parts onto pallets.

    Station arm, mobile two-arm

In the field.

Solar sites are built and kept running by hand, in heat and dust, far from where people want to work.

Concept render

  • Solar panel cleaning

    Cleans panel rows on dusty sites, in the heat of the day.

    Field robot

  • Solar panel installation

    Lifts panels and places them on their mounting rails with two arms.

    Field robot
