Four bodies. One brain.

The hardware changes with the job. The software that learns the job is the same on all four.

Station arm

In development

A fixed arm for one station. It tends a machine, picks and places parts, and presents them for inspection.

Why this shape
It bolts down beside the machine it serves, so it is the simplest robot to install and the steadiest.
Jobs
Machine tending, pick and place, inspection
Works
Beside one machine

Trolley arm

In development

An arm on a trolley that one person rolls to whichever machine needs it.

Why this shape
One robot serves several machines, and nothing on your floor has to change.
Jobs
Machine tending and inspection across several machines
Works
Any machine on a flat shop floor

Mobile two-arm

In development

Moves between machines on its own and works with both hands: one arm holds, the other works.

Why this shape
Two arms can assemble, and wheels let one robot carry parts from station to station.
Jobs
Assembly, kitting, moving parts, palletizing
Works
Across the shop floor

Field robot

In development

A rugged two-armed machine for outdoor work, built for heat, dust and uneven ground.

Why this shape
Outdoor sites cannot be tidied for a robot, so the robot has to cope with the site.
Jobs
Solar panel cleaning and installation
Works
Outdoor sites
