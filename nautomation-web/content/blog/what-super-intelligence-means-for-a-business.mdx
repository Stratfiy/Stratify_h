On 29 September 2026 the leading AI companies put always-on agents on stage and used the phrase "super intelligence" a lot. If you run a business, you probably had two reactions. The first: this sounds important. The second: what am I supposed to do on Monday?

Here is the plain-words version.

## What the phrase means

"Super intelligence" is a claim about capability. The models can now read, write, call, and act across many tools at once, for hours, without a person typing each step. That is the change. It is not a new kind of employee. It is software that can do work that used to need a person's attention, and can do a lot of it in parallel.

## What it changes for a business

Three things, in order of how soon they matter.

1. **Work that is defined can be done by an agent.** Chasing a quote. Matching an invoice to a purchase order. Answering a front-desk call and booking a slot. If you can write the job down, an agent can be set up to do it.
2. **The bottleneck moves from doing to approving.** When agents do the work, your people's job becomes deciding what gets sent, spent, or deleted. That is a different job, and it needs a different screen: an approval, not a to-do list.
3. **You need a record.** If an agent sent a supplier an email, you need to know it did, when, and who approved it. On the same day as the announcements, the same companies signed an accord that safe AI means controls and audits. Read that as: the record is not optional.

## What it does not change

- **Your accountability.** If an agent sends a wrong purchase order, the supplier will still call you. Approvals exist so that never happens without a person saying yes.
- **Your data's ownership.** Agents should work from your own email, WhatsApp and apps, under your accounts. If a vendor asks you to move everything into their system, ask why.
- **The need to start small.** One task, one channel, one team. Prove it on your data, then expand.

## What to do on Monday

Pick the one job your team complains about most. Write down what "done" looks like. Then get someone to set an agent up to do it, with approvals before anything is sent, and a record you can export.
